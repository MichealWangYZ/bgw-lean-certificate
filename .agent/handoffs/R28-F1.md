`#print axioms` for `sigA_common`, `sigA_compat`, `MF.blk_ok`, `compat_st_unique`,
`compat_energy_shift`, `coulomb_same`, `omT_dens`, `Espec_q0_input` and `compat_self` gives
`[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` for each.

**Why the predicate is not trivial.** `Compat` does not just restate the old theorem's
inputs. It equates the blocks' Inputs, `gl` and `st` with the images of ONE system. The
theory side of `sigA_compat` is computed from that system, not from `bs`. `inp_pins` shows
that a `q ≠ 0` block determines every grid point's sphere, coefficient rows and energies.
Two consequences follow:
* `st`, which reads only those, is determined by `bs` (`compat_st_unique`);
* the reviewer's case (fixed `bs`, changed `st` energies) is refuted for every system
  (`compat_energy_shift`).

`compat_st_unique` and `compat_energy_shift` need three conditions: `plus` maps the grid
onto itself, `k0` is a grid point, and every `k0 - q_t` is a grid point.

## Target 2 result (WFN vs WFN_inner)

`python3 bgw/r28_f1/check.py --out bgw/r28_f1/data/check.json` takes 15 s. It defaults to
`~/bgw-src/runs/eps` and `~/bgw-src/runs/sig-pristine`. A fresh run (`--run DIR`)
reproduces the same numbers to float noise.
* **Agree:**
  * the cell, symmetry, atoms and G list are identical bitwise across WFN, WFNq and
    WFN_inner;
  * `nocc` is 4 on both sides, and `number_bands` is 18 on both sides;
  * the Hamiltonian is the same: a local V fitted on WFN_inner (kinetic scale
    1 within 2e-15, residual ≤ 4e-15 Ry) reproduces WFN's energies to ≤ 2.4e-7 meV and WFNq's to
    ≤ 6.9e-8 meV, with subspace overlap ≥ 1 - 3e-15.
* **Mismatch (finding):** the k-sets are DISJOINT.
  * Epsilon sums over the 32-point closure of the shifted grid (½,½,½).
  * Σ uses the unshifted 8-point grid.
  * There are 0 shared points; the minimum distance is ¼.
  * No state is shared, so coefficients and energies cannot be compared state by state.
  * Screening and Σ are two different finite systems of one Hamiltonian, so `Compat`
    fails for every system.
* **DensOK fails.**
  * RHO is the unsymmetrised IBZ sum (replayed to 1.1e-14).
  * It differs from the Σ system's own density by up to 0.318, and from the eps system's
    density by up to 0.343.
  * The star-averaged RHO equals the Σ system's density to 2.9e-14. So BRIDGE-Q0 row Q-6
    (+0.375 meV on O1) is the size of this failure.
* The q0 block's partners `k + q0` are WFNq states, not WFN grid states.
