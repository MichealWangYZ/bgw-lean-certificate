## Key theorems

* `sigAall_static_split`: `SigAall = Σ_q (Xq + Σ Pq) + tailD + CORall`. The static
  remainder of the all-band Σ is kept.
* `cor_q_decomp`, **`cor_all_decomp`**: under `CompleteK κ` alone,
  `CORall = Σ_q (corScale κ + corShift + corGch)`, where
  * `corScale κ = (κ-1) Σ w cloP`,
  * `corShift = Σ w (cloP - ρ_aa)`,
  * `corGch = Σ w [∉ Gch] ρ_aa`,
  * `w_ij = v_i IeU(I_ji)/4`.
* Vanishing conditions:
  * `corScale_one` (`κ = 1`);
  * `corShift_eq_zero` (ShiftClosed on pairs with `|I| ≥ TOL_Small`; via `amp_sum_shift`,
    which needs no completeness);
  * `corGch_eq_zero` (the `hch` of `chpart_sum_eq_exact`);
  * `cor_all_eq_zero`, `sigAall_of_closure` (all three together).
* Relation to the old closure theorem:
  * `cor_decomp_complete`, `COR_decomp_complete`: `Bloch.Complete` alone leaves
    `COR = Σ(corShift + corGch)`;
  * `cor_eq_zero_of_closure`: re-derives `COR = 0` from `hc`, `hs`, `hch`;
  * `corAll_of_state`: for a larger dyadic state, `CORall` is that state's `COR`.
