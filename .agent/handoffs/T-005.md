## Numerical findings

Sign convention: `Delta = generic candidate - near-pole candidate`, the
orientation of `GW.GPP.BGW4.branch_mismatch`. That theorem was used only as a
comparison target and is unchanged.

**Predicate structure.** `wdiffr > gamma^2` is `|d| > 0.5`;
`delwr < 1/(4*TOL_Small)` is `|d| > 2*sqrt(TOL_Small)*|wtilde|`. With the
defaults the second conjunct is redundant whenever `|wtilde| <= 250`, so the
sweep uses `wtilde = 1` (cases `A+`, `A-`, surface `|d| = 0.5`) and
`wtilde = 300` (case `B+`, surface `d = 0.6`, decided by `delwr`). `wtilde=300`
is a numerical construction to separate the surfaces, not a physical claim.

Each surface was located by bisecting on binary64 bit patterns (49, 51 and 44
steps) until the last predicate-false and first predicate-true `wxt` were
adjacent representable numbers. No decimal value is described as the boundary.

**The machine surface is not always the decimal one.** For `A+` the last false
point is exactly `wxt = 1.5`: `d*d = 0.25` equals `limittwo` exactly and the
strict `>` fails. For `A-` the subtraction `wdiff = wxt - wtilde` is outside the
Sterbenz range, so the last false point is one ULP *below* `wxt = 0.5` and still
reports `d = -0.5` exactly; the surface is displaced by the rounding of the
subtraction, not by the comparison.

**Full occupation, item 4.** One-ULP jump across the surface versus the largest
adjacent-point step that stays inside one branch:

| case | jump | within-branch | local \|value\| | jump/scale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `A+` | 5.55e-17 | 2.22e-16 | 0.200 | 2.8e-16 |
| `A-` | 5.55e-17 | 2.22e-16 | 0.333 | 1.7e-16 |
| `B+` | 9.80e-13 | 6.57e-12 | 0.2498 | 3.9e-12 |

In every case the branch change costs **less** than ordinary within-branch
variation, so the continuity expectation held and no tolerance was tuned. The
`B+` scale is five orders coarser because `-ssx + 0.5*sch` there subtracts two
numbers of size ~250 to make ~0.25; that conditioning is reported rather than
hidden, and a test asserts the two regimes separately.

**Half occupation, item 4.** The same one-ULP step becomes a finite
discontinuity: `A+` `+0.500`, `A-` `-0.500`, `B+` `+125.0`, each matching the
`branch_mismatch` closed form at those inputs (residual 0, 0 and 4.7e-13, i.e.
<= 3.8e-15 relative). The sign follows the side of the mode, because
`Omega2/((x-wt)(x+wt))` changes sign across `x = wt` while
`Omega2/(2*wt*(x+wt))` does not. This is a discrepancy between the two formulas
the source selects between, in a dimensionless local accumulator. **Not a
material self-energy error.**

**Occupation linearity, item 5.** Four fixed nonsingular points away from
overflow and from the `sexcut` clamp, `occ = 1, 0.75, 0.5, 0.25`:

| point | `ieps` | `wtilde` | `wxt` | `Delta/(1-occ)` | max rel. spread | `Delta(1)` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 2.0 | 5.0000e-01 | 2.2e-16 | 2.8e-17 |
| P2 | 0.37 | 2.50 | 4.0 | 3.0833e-01 | 5.4e-16 | -5.6e-17 |
| P3 | -0.80 | 1.70 | 0.3 | 4.8571e-01 | 1.3e-15 | -1.7e-16 |
| P4 | 2.00 | 0.75 | 3.0 | 3.3333e-01 | 3.3e-16 | 0 |

The ratio is constant to at most 1.3e-15 relative (under 8 ULP, which is the
justified tolerance: three evaluations differing only by the rounding of
`1-occ` and of two three-term sums), and `Delta(1)` sits at the rounding floor
rather than exactly zero. Measured `Delta(occ)` agrees with the closed form to
<= 1.4e-15 relative. The Fortran computes the same ratios independently and the
Python analyser reproduces them bit-for-bit.

**Item 6 invariances.** A common energy-zero shift `E -> E+C, eps -> eps+C`,
applied before the binary64 subtraction exactly as `mtxel_cor.f90:1744` builds
`wxt`, leaves the result bit-identical for `C` up to 1e16 and fails at 1e17,
where `(E+C)-(eps+C)` rounds to 0 and `wxt` hits the `TOL_Zero` clamp. That is
a limit of constructing the input, not an asymmetry of the kernel, and it is
reported rather than avoided. Scaling `I_eps` by 2, 1/4, -1, -3.5 scales both
candidates and their discrepancy by the same factor to <= 3.2e-16 relative.

**Secondary.** Branch 3 needs `delwr <= TOL_Zero` while `wdiffr <= limittwo`,
i.e. `|wtilde| <= 5e-7`; it is reached in the driver only with `wtilde = 0`,
which `mtxel_cor.f90:774` already `cycle`s away before this loop.

## Claim boundaries

- This is a standalone driver executing a source-faithful extraction. It is
  **not a BerkeleyGW run** and establishes **no production-material impact**.
- Values are per-`(ig,igp)` dimensionless local values of `-occ*ssx + 0.5*sch`
  with `aqsntemp*MYCONJG(aqsmtemp)*vcoul(igp)` divided out.
- Continuity at `occ = 1` is a floating-point observation about this driver at
  these inputs, not a proof and not an accuracy claim about BerkeleyGW.
- The half-occupation discontinuity is a difference between two formulas. It
  becomes physical only for a run that reaches partial occupation in a
  self-consistent state, which this project has not demonstrated (T-003).
- Agreement with `branch_mismatch` is a binary64 comparison against a
  real-number theorem, not a verification of the theorem or of the Fortran.
- The hand transcription remains a human reading of the source; the digest
  check constrains which bytes were read, not that they were read correctly.
