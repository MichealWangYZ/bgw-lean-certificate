## Input

- a completeness critic;

Its summary is recorded in `docs/reviews/R28-R2.md` (written by this task). It found no
BLOCKER and the following open MAJOR items:

- **M1.** `Compat` did not require the Σ state `k0` and `k0 - q_t` to be grid points,
  so REVIEW-28 F1's adversarial case (same screening, arbitrary Σ energies) still
  satisfied it through an off-grid index.
- **M2.** `MF.TR` is an exact ordered-list time-reversal identity, and it cannot hold at
  a time-reversal-invariant grid point. The F1 common-system theorem therefore cannot
  derive the screening for O1's X/L states. This was undisclosed.
- **M3.** The theory side shares many specification definitions beyond Ω² (`Xq`,
  `quad`/`xS`/`pd`, `vS`, `vC`). `docs/BRIDGE.md` nevertheless said "derived
  independently of the code", and three rows said "proved equal" (REVIEW-28 F2, fix
  incomplete).
- **M4.** The README-linked reviewer's guide `docs/review/index.html` predated the bridge
  and R28 and contradicted the corrected scope.

It also found about 25 MINOR items: evidence labels, decimals, stale records,
docstrings, and the generated-guide citations.
