# T-004-R1 provenance (audit repair)

Second revision of the T-004 evidence. PROVENANCE.txt is unchanged and
remains historically correct for the first submission. No BerkeleyGW
build or run was performed in this repair; the instrumented tree and all
raw fixtures are untouched.

## Corrections applied (audit findings 1, 2, 3, 5 and smaller items)
F1 the primary aggregate no longer sums SIGNED mismatches across
   unrelated outputs. Each output's magnitude is taken first:
   1.0559% (was 0.6731%). The cross-output figure is retained as a
   labelled diagnostic carrying a do-not-quote warning.
F2 units corrected to intermediate Rydberg, not dimensionless; the
   missing outer factors are listed per output type, because 1/ndeg
   applies to diagonal elements only (shiftenergy.f90:69 loops over
   sig%ndiag) and offdiagonals take ryd at sigma_main.f90:1860-1861.
F3 signed_over_local was overwritten by the reconstruction residual
   before emission; call 107 reported 8.08e-16 instead of 2524.16.
F5 the accumulator's own band/il/spin/icalc/qk are now checked against
   the identity its records claim; a mismatch is rejected.
--max-groups no longer changes the global statistics.
G=G'=0 identification for record 1970 removed: ig/igp are sorted-list
   indices and the export contains no G-vectors.
