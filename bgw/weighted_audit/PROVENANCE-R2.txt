  run's tally, validates it with the T-003 checker's tally logic
  unmodified, and requires the export to equal its near-pole occ != 1
  count. A tally altered while preserving row sums is rejected.

  diagnostic, the stale 'dimensionless' open question is corrected, the
  population/sample relationship is stated, the two release gates are
  separated, and the correction list is extended to fifteen entries.
