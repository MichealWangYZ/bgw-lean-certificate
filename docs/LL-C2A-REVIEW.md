## The scientific step is substantive

`StructuralRing.lean` defines `ringProps`, `ringInts`, and `ring k` as graph data.
`ring_zero/one/two` recover the previous graph data, not just numerical values.
`contraction_ring` is derived by peeling assignments and proving a recurrence
for the graph sum, then connecting it to `v * (bubble G * v)^k`.

The automorphism proof actually replaces enumeration. It walks from fixed extI
to a_0, across its propagators to b_0, then along the next interaction; induction
fixes all sites. Distinct interaction edges force their permutation to be the
identity. Each pair of parallel propagators can then independently swap.
`autEquivBits` includes explicit inverse laws; `card_aut_ring` follows from it.
The use of `decide` to read a Boolean swap bit is NOT per-order group enumeration.

`RingHalfEdge.lean` separately defines individual flags, edge mates and site
incidence. Its admissible permutations are not restricted by fiat to bubble
swaps. It re-proves rigidity, builds `ringHalfEdgeEquivBits`, and composes with
`autEquivBits`; `ringHalfEdgeEquivAut_bits` identifies the physical swap choices.
This satisfies the ring-specific correspondence target. The result concerns the
defined finite types/interpretation, not a universal formalization of Definition 3.8.

These are materially stronger results than three small cardinality checks. I do
not find an added hypothesis that assumes the central result or an enumeration
substitute masquerading as the general proof. That assessment is a source review,
