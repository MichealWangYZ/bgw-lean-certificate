## What the old `CompleteK` result covers, and what it does not

`GW/Bridge/Cor.lean` (`sigA_all_bands`, `sigT_all_bands` and their energy steps) assumes
`CompleteK κ`: the stored dyadic computed rows `R` and completion rows `C` satisfy
`RᵀR + CᵀC = κ·1`. `bgw/bridge_cor/cor.py` takes `κ` to be a Gershgorin bound above
`λ_max(RᵀR)`.

* **Covered.** The enclosure is a correct theorem about any real completion `C` that
  satisfies this scaled identity. Any number of rows is allowed. It is also a correct
  statement about larger dyadic states (`sigAall_of_state`, `completeK_of_complete`).
* **Not covered.** For `κ > λ_max(RᵀR)` the defect `κ·1 - RᵀR` is positive definite and
  has rank `L`, so `C` needs at least `L` rows. The physical omitted bands of an
  `L`-dimensional basis are `L - N_b` rows. The true finite-basis eigenvectors are
  therefore never an instance.
  * Witness: `R = [1, 0]`, `κ = 1 + 2^-40`. It is formalised as `f3_witness` (no single
    row works). `f3_witness_one` shows that at `κ = 1` the row `[0, 1]` works.
  * The `κ` in `cor.json` does not certify the true omitted bands, and the BRIDGE-COR
    table is a statement about an abstract scaled-frame completion.

## The new statement (`GW/Bridge/CorPerturb.lean`, `GW/Bridge/CorPerturbLowdin.lean`)

At each `q_t`, let `R` be the stored rows (`Rst`, `N_b × L`, read as exact reals), and let
`η_t = ‖R Rᵀ - 1‖_F²` (`gramDef`).

1. **Löwdin** (`Lowdin.lowdin`, `Lowdin.lowdin_nat`). If `η_t < 1`, there EXIST rows
   `P` with `P Pᵀ = 1` and `‖P - R‖_F² ≤ η_t`. The proof uses Löwdin's construction
   `R* = (R Rᵀ)^{-1/2} R`, for which `‖R* - R‖_F² = Σ_k (1 - √λ_k)² ≤ Σ_k (1 - λ_k)² = η_t`;
   the statement does not pin `P` to `R*`. So every row of `P` is within `δ_t = √η_t`
   (`exists_true_rows`, `RowDist`). The construction uses Mathlib's spectral
   theorem. Mathlib has no Löwdin orthogonalisation or polar decomposition of
   rectangular matrices.
2. **Completion with the physical row count** (`Lowdin.exists_completion_nat`,
   `exists_compl`). Exactly orthonormal `P` has `N_b ≤ L`. It is completed by exactly
   `L - N_b` rows to `PᵀP + CᵀC = 1`, with any prescribed omitted energies
   (`CompleteP`, the `κ = 1` identity).
3. **Enclosure.** Take any real rows `P` within `d_t` of the stored rows, and ANY
   completion `cp` of `P` with `CompleteP` and omitted bands at `x ≤ -D_t`. Let
   `SigAallP` / `SigTallP` be the all-band Σ of the system whose computed eigenvectors
   are `P`: the spec's or theory's formula with `P` on the computed bands and `cp` on the
   omitted ones.
   * `sigA_all_bands_true` (`D > ½`) and `sigA_diff_all_bands_true` (`D > 3/2`, the energy
     step): `|SigAallP - SigA - cenK(κ = 1)| ≤ radP + cenErr + compErrA`, with
     `2·compErrA` for the step.
   * `sigT_all_bands_true` and `sigT_diff_all_bands_true` give the same for the theory Σ.
   * `sigA_all_bands_lowdin` combines 1 to 3 with `d_t = √η_t`. It asserts exactly
     orthonormal rows `P` within `√η_t` of the stored rows, and gives both quantities of
     the linearised Eqp1 for every exact completion of that `P` (by exactly `L - N_b`
     rows, the physical count).

The centre is Cor's `cenK` at `κ = 1`, evaluated on the stored data. Every term below is
also computed from the stored data and `d`. Here `u_n(G) = M_n(G)` is the stored pair
density, `A_v(G)` the amplitudes of state `a`, and `e(G) = √(Σ_v A_v(G)²)·d_t` (`eP`).

* `mP_close` (Cauchy-Schwarz) gives `|M*_n(G) - M_n(G)| ≤ e(G)`.
* `prodErr` bounds the change of a product: `|x*y* - xy| ≤ e_x|y| + |x|e_y + e_x e_y`.
* `dT_ij = Σ_{n<N_b} prodErr(u_n(G_i), u_n(G_j), e_i, e_j)` bounds the change of the
  omitted pair sums `T_ij` (`tailP_close`).
* `radP = Σ |v_i| √(T_ii + dT_ii) √(T_jj + dT_jj) r_ji` is Cor's radius with `T_ii`
  inflated.
* `cenErr = Σ |v_i| dT_ij |c_ji|`.
* `compErrA` is the change of the computed bands, `xErr + Σ_ij |v_i| 751 |IeU_ji| dT_ij`,
  where the exchange term is `xErr = Σ_{n<nocc} Σ_g |v^x_g| (2 e_g |u_ng| + e_g²)`.
  * The per-mode bound `|ACq + Pq kernel| ≤ 751 |IeU|` is proved (`specKc_abs_le`): every
    branch of `schM` and `ssxM` is within `500 |IeU|`, because `LIM1 = 250000` and
    `sexcut = 4`.
  * For the theory kernel the bound `B` is a hypothesis, because `thK` has poles.

**Propagation term** = `radP - radK₁ + cenErr + compErr`, where `radK₁` is Cor's radius at
`κ = 1`. It is the whole difference from the `κ = 1` version of the BRIDGE-COR
enclosure. It is linear in `d` up to `O(d²)`.

## Remaining trust

* **Eigenvectors.** It is not proved that the rows `P` are the eigenvectors of the finite
  (EPM) Hamiltonian `H`, and the enclosure is not established for the physical omitted
  bands.
  * The rows `P` of `sigA_all_bands_lowdin` are exactly orthonormal; with a completion
    they are the eigenvectors of the surrogate `H* = Σ_n E_n P_n P_nᵀ + Σ_n ε_n C_n C_nᵀ`.
  * The physical omitted EPM eigenvectors complete the EPM's OWN eigenvectors, not `P`.
    Covering them needs `sigA_all_bands_true` with the distance hypothesis `RowDist`, at
    the distance `d` between the EPM eigenvectors and the stored rows.
  * That `d` needs `H`. The computable quantity is the residual `‖H Rᵀ - Rᵀ diag(E)‖_F`
    of the stored rows, plus the gap from band `N_b` to band `N_b + 1`. Davis-Kahan then
    gives `d ≤ residual / gap`. This is neither formalised nor evaluated here. At
    `N_b = 30` and `100` the cut splits an exact degeneracy (gap ≈ 2e-15 Ry), so this
    route needs care there; at `N_b = 18`, `60` and `168` the gaps are ≥ 0.0037 Ry.
* **State `a`.** The state `a` (at `k`) is the stored row throughout. This is the same
  input trust as E1.
* **Omitted energies.** They enter only through `x ≤ -D`, a hypothesis exactly as in
  `Cor.lean`.
