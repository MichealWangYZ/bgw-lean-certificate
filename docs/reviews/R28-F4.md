## The finding is correct

The reviewer is right. `Cor.SigAall = SigA + tailS` adds the omitted bands' `ACq + Pq` to
the run's Σ, and nothing in that definition makes the static part zero.
`sigAall_static_split` proves

    SigAall = Σ_q (Xq + Σ_n Pq) + tailD + CORall,
    CORall  = Σ_q (CSq + Σ_{n computed} ACq + Σ_{n omitted} ACq),

so the static remainder `CORall` is kept, not removed. Under `CompleteK κ`, and with no
other hypothesis, it splits exactly into three named terms (`cor_q_decomp`,
`cor_all_decomp`). Write `w_ij = v_i IeU(I_ji)/4`,
`cloP_ij = Σ_v A_v(G_i) A_v(G_j)` (the closure of the `k - q` sphere) and
`ρ_ij = ρ_aa(G_i - G_j)`. Then

| term | definition | vanishes when (theorem) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `corScale κ` (closure scale) | `(κ - 1) Σ_ij w_ij cloP_ij` | `κ = 1` (`corScale_one`) |
| `corShift` (shift-closure defect) | `Σ_ij w_ij (cloP_ij - ρ_ij)` | the `k - q` sphere is `ShiftClosed` for every pair with `\|I_ji\| ≥ TOL_Small` (`corShift_eq_zero`) |
| `corGch` (`Gch` coverage) | `Σ_ij w_ij [G_j - G_i ∉ Gch] ρ_ij` | `Gch` covers those differences, i.e. the `hch` of `chpart_sum_eq_exact` (`corGch_eq_zero`) |

All three conditions together give `CORall = 0` (`cor_all_eq_zero`). They are sufficient,
not necessary: in `nn_corAll_zero` (R28-R2, `GW/Bridge/CorResidual.lean`) all three fail
(`nn_scale_fails`, `nn_not_shiftClosed`, `nn_gch_misses`) and `CORall = 0`. When all three hold, `SigAall` is the
exchange plus the GPP parts of all bands (`sigAall_of_closure`).
`corShift` and `corGch` use no band at `k - q`. They are fixed by the state `a`, the
spheres, `eps⁻¹` and `v`, so adding bands never removes them.

**Relation to the existing theorems.** For a state whose own rows satisfy `Bloch.Complete`
(the `hc` of `chpart_sum_eq_exact`), only the scale term drops out:
`COR = Σ_q (corShift + corGch)` (`cor_decomp_complete`, `COR_decomp_complete`). The
`hs` and `hch` hypotheses of `chpart_sum_eq_exact` / `sigA_eq_theory_complete` are exactly
what removes the other two terms (`cor_eq_zero_of_closure`, which re-derives `COR = 0`
through the decomposition). For the completion made of a larger dyadic state's further
rows, `CORall` is that state's own `COR` (`corAll_of_state`). In particular, the
168-band diagnostic carries the 168-band state's static remainder.

**Witnesses (Lean).**
* The reviewer's example (`wit_*`): WFN sphere `{0}` with `c(0) = 1`, eps sphere `{0, b}`,
  unit weights, `I_eps = diag(0, ½)` and `0 ∈ Gch`. The bands are complete
  (`wit_complete`), yet `COR = -1/8` (`wit_cor`). All of it is shift-closure defect:
  `corShift = -1/8` and `corGch = 0` (`wit_shift`, `wit_gch`), and the sphere is not
  shift-closed (`wit_not_shiftClosed`).
* The scaled closure (`sc_*`): one unit channel with `I_eps = c` and `|c| ≥ TOL_Small`,
  one computed band and one completion row `r`. This satisfies `CompleteK (1 + r²)`
  (`sc_completeK`) and gives `CORall = r²c/4 = (κ - 1)c/4` (`sc_corAll`). Here
  `corShift = corGch = 0` (`sc_scale`).

These show that the `κ = 1` condition and the shift-closure condition cannot simply be
dropped: with the other two terms zero, a failure of either can leave a nonzero
remainder. No witness is given for the `Gch` coverage condition.
