  outward or marked ≈.

Lean:

  fixes `k` and the sphere at `k` holds a nonzero vector, the hypotheses of

  has no k-coordinates.
- **`hyps_unsat_of_unpaired` is restated with its actual premise `hnp`.** The inference
  "a sphere determines k" was invalid, and PR #30 removed it.
- `bgw/r28_f1/check.py` now checks `hnp` on the Si-EPM Σ grid. For X and L, no grid
  point stores the negated sphere even as a set, so `hnp` holds there for any ordering.
- Comment-only corrections in 13 `GW/Bridge` files:
  - uniqueness branches;
  - nonsingularity for `screened_entry`, `rpa_neumann` and `wDyn_tendsto`, and the
    totalized `Etheory`;
  - dropped hypotheses and totalizations;
  - PR #30's garbled sentence.
